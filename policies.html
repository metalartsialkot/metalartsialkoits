📦 Shipping Policy

Domestic Shipping (Pakistan)

We offer FREE shipping across all Pakistan. Orders are typically delivered within 2-5 business days depending on your location.

  • Karachi: 1-2 business days
  • Lahore/Islamabad: 2-3 business days
  • Other cities: 3-5 business days
  • Remote areas: 5-7 business days

International Shipping

We ship worldwide! International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination:

  • UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar: 3-5 business days
  • UK, Europe: 5-7 business days
  • USA, Canada: 7-10 business days
  • Australia: 7-10 business days

🚚 FREE Shipping on all orders within Pakistan!

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 24-48 hours after confirmation. You will receive a tracking number via WhatsApp and email once your order is shipped.

🔄 Return & Refund Policy

7-Day Return Guarantee

We offer a 7-day return policy from the date of delivery. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, you can return it for a full refund or exchange.

Conditions for Return

  • Item must be in original condition
  • Original packaging must be intact
  • Product should not be damaged
  • Return must be initiated within 7 days

Refund Process

Once we receive the returned item, we will inspect it and process your refund within 3-5 business days. Refunds will be issued to the original payment method.

✅ 5-Year Warranty on all products against manufacturing defects!

Damaged Items

If your item arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours with photos of the damage. We will replace the item at no additional cost.
